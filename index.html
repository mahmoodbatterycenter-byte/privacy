Privacy Policy

Applies to the Master Blaster Block Android app. Last updated: 26 October 2026.

Master Blaster Block is an offline puzzle game. It does not collect, store on a server, sell, or share any personal information. There is no account, no sign-in, and no contact with any server at any time.

What the app stores

Your high scores, streak, sound and theme settings, and unlocked power-ups are saved only on your own phone, in the app's private storage on that device. They never leave the phone. Uninstalling the app deletes them permanently.

What the app does not do

  • No account, sign-in, name, email, or phone number is requested or needed.
  • No location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or files are read.
  • No advertising SDK and no analytics SDK are present, so no advertising ID is used.
  • No data is sent over the network. The game is fully playable with mobile data and Wi-Fi switched off.
  • Nothing is shared with, or sold to, any third party.

Why the app lists the INTERNET permission

The app is built on a WebView component, which requires this permission to load the game's own files that are already inside the app. The app never uses the internet connection, and it works the same with the connection turned off.

Children

The game is intended for a general audience and collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes
